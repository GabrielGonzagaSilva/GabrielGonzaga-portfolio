Overview

The problem

Information and operational resources were fragmented across tools, increasing friction in access, monitoring and decision support.

The outcome

A working internal platform now centralizes key information, automates recurring updates and combines automation with human validation.

Outcome & learnings

Impact

The Hub is operational, supports recurring automated updates and consolidates multi-market monitoring in one internal product.

What I learned

AI-enabled products need more than automation: reliability, governance, fallbacks and clear human validation are part of the product experience.
